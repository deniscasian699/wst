This Privacy Policy explains how **WST** ("the app", "we", "us") handles information on Android. The app is developed by **Denis Casian**, an independent developer based in Romania.

## 1. Privacy Summary

- The app contains **no third-party advertising SDK** and no behavioral ad tracking.
- Core app content is stored and processed locally whenever possible.
- No app account is required.
- We do not sell personal information or share it for targeted advertising.
- Google Play and RevenueCat process limited purchase information for optional products.

## 2. Information Stored Locally

The app may store the following information in private app storage:

- Download, upload, ping, jitter and packet-loss results
- Loaded-latency samples and selected endpoint
- Test timestamp and local history
- Test duration, server and app settings
- Cached Supporter and Premium status

This content is used only to provide app functionality. We do not intentionally upload it to a developer-operated server. Local data normally remains until you delete it, clear app data or uninstall the app.

## 3. Purchases and Subscriptions

Optional products are processed by **Google Play Billing** and managed with **RevenueCat**. Depending on the transaction, these providers may process:

- An anonymous RevenueCat App User ID
- App package, app version, platform and technical diagnostics
- Product IDs, purchase tokens, transaction dates and entitlement status
- Subscription renewal, expiration, refund, revocation and restoration status
- Store country and currency information
- Information required for fraud prevention and purchase validation

We do not receive full payment-card details. Google Play and RevenueCat act under their own privacy policies:

- [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
- [RevenueCat Privacy Policy](https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy/)


## 4. Diagnostic Endpoints and IP Addresses

Internet testing requires direct connections to external endpoints. As part of normal internet communication, those providers may receive an IP address, user agent, request time and transferred data volume. We do not control their independent server logs.

WST does not operate a server that receives or stores personal speed-test history. CSV export is initiated by the user and is shared only through destinations the user selects.


## 5. Permissions and System Access

| Permission / access | Purpose | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| `Internet / network state` | Run diagnostic tests and verify purchases | Test endpoints receive normal connection metadata. |
| `Google Play Billing` | Optional Supporter and Premium purchases | Payment details stay with Google. |
| `File creation / share sheet` | User-initiated Premium CSV export | The user chooses where to save or share the file. |
| `Launcher component state` | Apply eligible alternative icons | Only after user selection. |

Permissions are used only for the described app functions. They are not used for advertising or cross-app tracking.

## 6. Third-Party Services

| Service | Role |
|---|---|
| Google Play Billing | Purchase processing, localized pricing, refunds and subscription management. |
| RevenueCat | Anonymous entitlement verification and restoration. |
| Diagnostic endpoints | Cloudflare, Tele2, Bouygues Telecom, Online.net, GitHub CDN, Google CDN or other listed targets may receive IP address and standard request metadata. |

The app does not include Google AdMob, Firebase Analytics, Google Analytics or another advertising analytics SDK unless a future policy explicitly states otherwise.

## 7. How Information Is Used

Information is processed only to:

- Provide the app’s requested features
- Save preferences and app content locally
- Verify and restore optional purchases
- Prevent purchase fraud and diagnose entitlement failures
- Open external links selected by the user
- Respond to support or bug reports submitted voluntarily

## 8. Legal Bases

Where GDPR, UK GDPR or similar law applies, processing may rely on:

- **Contract performance** — providing app functionality and purchased benefits
- **Legitimate interests** — security, fraud prevention, troubleshooting and reliable operation
- **Consent or user request** — optional permissions, notifications, exports and external links
- **Legal obligations** — payment, tax, accounting and consumer-protection requirements handled by applicable providers

## 9. Your Choices and Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to processing. You can also:

- Use the app without creating an account
- Revoke optional permissions in Android Settings
- Delete local content inside the app where available
- Clear app data or uninstall the app to remove local storage
- Manage or cancel subscriptions through Google Play
- Use Restore Purchases for eligible products

Because most app content is stored only on your device, we may not possess a server-side copy that we can access, export or delete for you. Purchase records controlled by Google Play or RevenueCat are subject to those providers’ processes.

## 10. U.S. State Privacy Disclosures

We do not sell personal information and do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. Residents of jurisdictions such as California, Virginia, Colorado, Connecticut or Utah may have rights to know, access, correct or delete applicable information and to opt out of sales or targeted sharing. No such sale or targeted sharing is performed by the app.

## 11. Children’s Privacy

The app is a general-audience utility and is not designed to collect personal information from children. It contains no targeted advertising and does not require a user profile. Optional purchases are controlled by Google Play and device or family payment settings.

## 12. Data Retention

- Local content remains until changed, deleted, app data is cleared or the app is uninstalled.
- Purchase and subscription records are retained by Google Play and RevenueCat under their legal and operational requirements.
- Support information voluntarily submitted through email, GitHub or the bug tracker is retained only as needed to respond and maintain records.

## 13. International Processing

Google and RevenueCat may process purchase-related and technical information in countries other than your own. They are responsible for the transfer safeguards described in their respective privacy documentation.

## 14. Security

We use reasonable measures such as Android app sandboxing, minimal data collection and HTTPS for external SDK communication. No device, network, store or online service can be guaranteed completely secure.

## 15. External Links

The app may open Google Play, policy pages, subscription management, support, bug-report or other user-selected destinations. Those services have their own terms and privacy practices and may receive normal connection metadata such as IP address and browser information.

## 16. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy when app functionality, providers or legal requirements change. The “Last updated” date identifies the current version. Material changes will be communicated where required by law.

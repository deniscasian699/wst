## 2. Information we process

### 2.1 Network and speed test data

When you run a speed test, the Application processes technical measurements required to perform the diagnostic:

- **Download Speed (Mbps)** — the rate at which your device receives data from a test endpoint;

- **Upload Speed (Mbps)** — the rate at which your device sends test data to an upload endpoint;

- **Ping / Latency (ms)** — estimated response time to diagnostic endpoints;

- **Loaded Latency** — latency samples collected during download and upload phases to show connection behaviour under load;

- **Jitter (ms)** — variation between latency samples;

- **Ping Loss (ICMP, %)** — unanswered probes in a 20-probe sample to 1.1.1.1, separate from the HTTP speed-test server. No replies, incomplete sampling or unsupported ICMP is shown as unavailable, not confirmed 100% loss;

- **Test Server Information** — selected endpoint name, location, country and domain;

- **Timestamp** — date and time when the test was executed.

**Local History:** Your speed test history is stored locally on your device using local app storage. We do not operate a server that receives or stores your personal speed test history.

### 2.2 Device and app information

The Application may process limited technical information necessary for app functionality and compatibility, such as:

- To perform internet speed tests, WiFi diagnostics and latency measurements;

- To save your selected settings locally, such as server choice and test duration;

- To verify Supporter, subscription and Premium access through RevenueCat and Google Play Billing;

**No Selling of Data:** We do not sell, rent or trade your local speed test history or personal information to third parties.

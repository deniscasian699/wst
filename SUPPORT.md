## 🔧 Common Issues & Solutions

### 🌐 Server Returned HTTP 429 / Test Could Not Finish

- Select **Auto** so the app can validate and choose another available endpoint.
- A 429 means the provider is rate-limiting requests. The app respects its cooldown;
  repeatedly starting tests is not a way to bypass it.
- If every suitable endpoint is unavailable, wait before trying again.
- Check internet access, captive portals and VPN restrictions.

### 📊 Results Differ Between Devices

Wi-Fi hardware, signal strength, background transfers, routing and public
server load can change results. Test the same endpoint under similar conditions
and avoid concurrent speed tests. The results are estimates, not guaranteed
contractual measurements.

### 📉 Ping Loss Is Unavailable

Loss uses a separate 20-probe ICMP sample to 1.1.1.1. Some devices or networks
block ICMP or do not support the required command. No replies or incomplete
sampling is unavailable rather than confirmed 100% loss. HTTP test failures
are not substituted as packet-loss measurements.

### 🗂️ History and CSV Export

History stays on the device: up to 10 recent results free, or 50 with Premium
Lifetime. CSV export requires Premium Lifetime. Uninstalling or clearing app
data can remove local history; save a CSV first if you need a separate copy.

### 💎 Purchase Not Activating

1. Sign in to Google Play with the account used for purchase.
2. Use **Restore Purchases** in the app's purchase screen.
3. Allow time for purchase status to synchronise and reopen the app.
4. If access is still missing, email support with the Google Play order ID privately.

### 🔔 How to Remove Ads

**Supporter One-Time** removes all advertising while the purchase entitlement
remains valid. **Monthly Supporter** removes advertising while active.

**Premium Lifetime does not remove ads.** It unlocks advanced diagnostics,
loaded-latency charts, longer history, CSV export, a badge and eligible icons.
Purchasing it does not grant ownership of Supporter products.

Refund, revocation or subscription expiry can restore ads after purchase status
synchronises. Earlier data held by advertising providers is not automatically deleted.

### ⚙️ Advertising Privacy Choices

Use the advertising privacy entry in **Settings** while ads are enabled to review
available AdMob / UMP choices. Availability depends on regional requirements and
configured privacy messages. The entry is hidden when ads are removed.
Declining personalisation does not necessarily remove permitted non-personalised ads.

### ❤️ Monthly Supporter After Buying Premium Lifetime

These are different products. Premium Lifetime does not cancel Monthly Supporter.
Manage subscriptions through
[Google Play subscriptions](https://play.google.com/store/account/subscriptions).

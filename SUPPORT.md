## Common Questions

### Results are lower than expected

Move closer to the router, stop other traffic, test another endpoint and compare multiple runs.

### Ping or jitter is unstable

Use Ethernet if available, pause downloads and compare Wi-Fi with mobile data.

### A server fails

Choose another endpoint. Third-party endpoints may be unavailable or rate-limited.

### CSV export is missing

CSV export requires the applicable Premium entitlement and saved test history.

### Premium did not restore

Use Restore Purchases with the original Google account.

## Billing and Refunds

Billing is handled by Google Play. Manage subscriptions at [Google Play Subscriptions](https://play.google.com/store/account/subscriptions). Refund requests are governed by [Google Play’s refund policy](https://support.google.com/googleplay/answer/2479637).

Never post payment details, full order receipts, private QR content, reminder text, travel notes or other sensitive information in a public issue.
